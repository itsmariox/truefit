# Design system: "Quiet" (direction D)

Status: DECIDED by the owner, 2026-11-17 (see PHASE4.md, step 4b). Source of truth for values: `tokens.json`. The mock-ups are on the private canvas named in PHASE4.md. Every later screen uses these tokens and components; a new colour, size or component is added HERE first.

## Character
A calm, mostly grey app in the spirit of a modern desktop assistant: a narrow icon rail, a list sidebar, a very dark (or very white) main panel, one large rounded input, thin line icons, soft rounded corners, almost no colour. Colour is kept for meaning: green for good, amber for "check this", one soft blue for links. It is an inspiration, not a copy: our own name, mark and icons, and a layout that fits our content.

## Layout
- **Window:** a 38 px top bar (traffic lights, the app name, and on the right the Claude status line) over the Mac's translucent sidebar material; one source-list sidebar (232 px) and one opaque main area. (Replaced the rail and rounded panel on 2026-11-17: see the update at the end.)
- **Navigation:** the top of the sidebar holds four items with a small line icon each: New job, History, My profile, Settings. The selected one has a grey fill.
- **Sidebar jobs:** below the navigation, groups "Needs you" (paused or waiting for the user), "Today", "Earlier", each job one row: a verdict dot and a one-line title (company, role), truncated with an ellipsis; "Show more". The selected row has the `rowSelected` fill.
- **Main area:** content is centred in a column (640 px for a job, 600 px for the big input, 780 px for two cards). Heading centred on the empty states; left-aligned on a job.

## Type
System font only (SF on the Mac). Hero 34/500, page 27/600, brand 22/600, card title 18/600, body 15, small 14, label 13/600. UK English, sentence case, no all-caps except nowhere.

## Components
1. **Rail item**, 2. **Sidebar row** (with verdict dot, selected, waiting variants), 3. **Big input** (pill, plus button, text, round send button), 4. **Segmented pill** (Verdict / CV), 5. **Card** (radius 16, 1 px `line`; the chosen card uses `line2`), 6. **Verdict pill** (icon plus word: Apply, Long shot, Skip) with the **fit bar** and percentage, 7. **Reason list** (tick or warning icon plus one sentence), 8. **Rule chip** (OK or "check" with icon), 9. **Primary button** (pill, `send` fill, 44 px high), 10. **Secondary button** (pill, `fieldEdge` outline), 11. **Field** (pill, `fieldEdge` outline, label above), 12. **Checkbox with label**, 13. **Status line** (top right) and **banner** (a card with an icon and the message title and text from `prd/09-messages.md`, plus buttons from `ACTION_LABELS`), 14. **Empty state** (centred hero and one input), 15. **Confirmation dialog** (typed confirmation for deletes; added when Settings is built).

## Behaviour and states
Follow macOS light or dark by default (Settings can force one). Every control has hover, pressed, focus and disabled states; the focus ring is a 2 px `link` outline with 2 px offset, never removed. Motion is minimal (150 ms fades). The big input never accepts free-text chat: only an ad or a link (PRD 07). Verdict and state never rely on colour alone.

## Accessibility (checked, 2026-11-17)
Every text and background pair used was computed (WCAG 2.x): text on every panel is at least 12.6 to 1 (dark) and 14.9 to 1 (light); muted text at least 5.4 to 1 (dark) and 4.9 to 1 (light) on every surface; links, good and warning text at least 4.6 to 1 on their backgrounds; icons at least 3 to 1. **Two failures were found and fixed before the choice was recorded:** (1) outlined buttons and form fields used an edge of about 2 to 1 against their background (fails the 3 to 1 needed to see a control's edge); a new `fieldEdge` token (dark `#7A7A7A`, light `#767676`) is now at least 3.5 to 1 on every surface; (2) light-mode muted text on the window chrome was 4.43 to 1; `muted` is now `#62626A` (4.98 to 1). **A documented exception:** the large hero input keeps the subtle `line` edge as in the mock-up; it is identified by its placeholder text, the plus button and the round send button.
Other rules: real buttons, links and labelled inputs (never a clickable div); icon-only buttons have an accessible name; touch targets at least 44 px; text scales with the system size.

## Update 2026-11-17: Truefit (the chosen brand direction C)
The product is now **Truefit**. Palette (the owner's): charcoal #26262B, coral #FF7A59, grey #4A4A52, off-white #F2F2F4. Coral is the only loud colour and has ONE job: the fit (the ring, the main button, the selected step). Text on coral is charcoal (5.6 to 1), never white. Light: white main area, charcoal text, hairlines at 16% charcoal. Dark: #1F1F23 main area, off-white text, coral keeps its value; the focus ring is charcoal in light and coral in dark.
Signature: the **fit ring** (a ring that fills to the percentage, number in tabular figures at its centre, one 900 ms sweep when a verdict appears, none under reduced motion) leads every verdict: score first, then the proof (rules, strengths, gaps). The **mark** (a ring with a tick) is the app icon, the menu-bar glyph and the sidebar brand. Verdict words always carry an icon (Apply, Long shot, Skip); colour is never the only signal. Primary button: coral, charcoal text, 600 weight; secondary: hairline outline.
**First-run guide:** four short, skippable steps (the promise with a sample verdict, how it stays honest, connect Claude, start your profile from a CV or from scratch), shown once; finished or skipped sets the setting "onboarding.done".
Native feel is kept: 13 px system type, 28 px controls, 6 px radii, the translucent sidebar. The override layers live at the end of `app/src/renderer/styles.css` (native pass, then Truefit). Where this note and earlier sections disagree, the newest note wins. Icons are generated by `app/scripts/make-icons.ts` (no design tool needed).

## Update 2026-11-17: motion (the working state)
One idea: **the ring at work**. While Claude works on a job, its card shows the Truefit ring with one coral arc that travels round it and breathes (it says "busy", never "x% done"). Beneath it, the REAL steps of the run appear as they begin and end (Reading the ad, Checking it against your rules, Writing your verdict; for a CV: choosing lines, laying out, checking every line): a small ring for the step in progress, a tick for each finished one. A job waiting its turn shows a still, grey arc. The sidebar dot of a job being worked on is a ring with a travelling coral segment. Entrances are 200 ms with the same ease as the verdict sweep. Under reduced motion nothing travels: the ring and the dot pulse gently (opacity) and the list appears without movement. Coral keeps its one job: the fit, now including "fit in progress".
